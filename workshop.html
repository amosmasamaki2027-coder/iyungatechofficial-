workshop

iyunga techical school is a place where students can learn and develop their skills in various technical fields. The school offers a range of workshops that cover topics such as programming, robotics, electronics, and more. These workshops are designed to provide hands-on experience and practical knowledge to help students succeed in their future careers. Whether you are a beginner or an advanced learner, there is a workshop for everyone at Iyunga Technical School. Join us and take the first step towards a brighter future!

  • Programming Workshop: Learn the basics of programming languages such as Python, Java, and C++.
  • Robotics Workshop: Explore the world of robotics and learn how to build and program robots.
  • Electronics Workshop: Gain hands-on experience with electronic circuits and components.
  • Web Development Workshop: Learn how to create websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Mobile App Development Workshop: Discover how to develop mobile applications for Android and iOS platforms.
